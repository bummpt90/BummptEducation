## 1. Introduction to the Authentication Gateway

BummptEducation is a secured, authenticated platform. When you visit the system:
- Unauthenticated visitors are directed to the **Authentication Gateway**.
- The Gateway allows authorized personnel to sign in, prospective educators to apply for account clearance, and registered users to initiate password recovery.
- Access to all academic records, student broadsheets, bursary ledgers, and institutional registries requires a verified session.

---

## 2. How to Request an Account (New Staff & Faculty)

If you are a new teacher, bursar, examination officer, or administrator joining a participating school:

1. On the **Authentication Gateway**, click the **Request Account** tab.
2. Complete your personal identity details:
   - **First Name**, **Middle Name** (optional), and **Surname**.
   - **Official Email Address** (must be active to receive communication).
   - **Phone Number** (Nigerian mobile format, e.g., `+234 803 123 4567`).
3. Select your institutional parameters:
   - **Assigned School Campus:** Select your institution from the dropdown menu (e.g., *Bummpt Model Academy*, *Government College Makurdi*).
   - **Desired Role:** Choose your primary functional role (`Teacher`, `Bursar`, `Examination Officer`, `Principal`).
4. Set a strong password (minimum 8 characters with letters, numbers, and symbols).
5. Click **Submit Account Request**.
6. **Important:** Your request is entered into the institutional queue with status **PENDING**. You cannot sign in immediately. Your school principal or the Ministry of Education administrator must verify your credentials before your account is activated.

---

## 3. How to Sign In & Manage Your Session

1. On the **Authentication Gateway**, select the **Sign In** tab.
2. Enter your registered **Email Address** and **Password**.
3. Click **Sign In to Dashboard**.
4. Upon successful verification:
   - You are redirected to your institutional dashboard tailored to your active role.
   - Your identity and role are displayed in the header bar.
5. **Signing Out:**
   - To end your session securely, click the red **Sign Out** button in the top navigation bar.
   - The system immediately revokes your session cookie and returns you to the Authentication Gateway.

---

## 4. Password Recovery Procedures

If you have forgotten your password:
1. Click the **Forgot Password** tab on the Authentication Gateway.
2. Enter your registered email address.
3. Click **Send Reset Instructions**.
4. The system will acknowledge your request. For security and anti-enumeration compliance, password reset confirmation notices are dispatched securely. If you do not receive an email within 15 minutes, please contact your school's designated administrator or ICT desk.

## 5. Principal Operational Guide: Reviewing Account Requests

As a School Principal or Vice Principal, you are responsible for vetting and approving account requests submitted for your campus:

1. Sign in to your verified Principal account.
2. Navigate to the **Admin & Operations** section from the top navigation.
3. In the sub-tabs toolbar, click **Account Requests**.
4. You will see the queue of pending staff requests specific to your school campus:
   - Review the applicant's name, email, phone number, and requested role.
   - Cross-reference with your physical staff registry and employment letters.
5. **To Approve a Request:**
   - Click the green **Approve** button.
   - Enter optional administrative notes (e.g., "Verified against Term 2 TRCN list").
   - Click **Confirm Approval**. The user account is immediately activated in the database, allowing the teacher to log in.
6. **To Reject a Request:**
   - Click the red **Reject** button.
   - Enter a mandatory **Rejection Reason** (e.g., "Applicant not listed on official school teaching roster").
   - Click **Confirm Rejection**. The request is marked rejected in the audit log.

## 6. Bursary Operational Guide: Fees, Invoicing & Receipts

1. **Setting Up Fee Structures:**
   - Navigate to **Admin Dashboard -> Bursary & Fees**.
   - Define termly amounts per class for Tuition, Development Levy, and Examinations.
2. **Generating Invoices:**
   - Invoices can be generated per student or in bulk for an entire class.
   - Invoices compute server-authoritative totals and balance due.
3. **Recording Payments & Printing Receipts:**
   - When a student or parent makes a bank deposit or cash payment, record the transaction under the student's invoice.
   - Once payment is verified, click **Print Digital Receipt** to generate a print-ready, verifiable receipt voucher with transaction reference numbers.

---

## 7. Academic Operational Guide: Assessments & Broadsheets

1. **Entering Continuous Assessment Scores:**
   - Navigate to **Academic Dashboard -> Broadsheet**.
   - Input marks for 1st CA (max 10), 2nd CA (max 10), Practical/Project (max 20), and Terminal Exam (max 60).
   - The engine automatically computes total scores, letter grades (A1 to F9), remarks, and GPA.
2. **Publishing Report Cards:**
   - Once all subject scores are reviewed by the Examination Officer and approved by the Principal, toggle the **Parent Download** publication status.
   - Parents can then view or download the student's report card from the Parent Portal.

## 8. Ministry & State Officer Guide: 23 LGAs Oversight

State Officers and Ministry Executives have statewide administrative clearance:
1. Navigate to **State HQ Command (23 LGAs)**.
2. Review real-time performance league tables across Makurdi, Gboko, Otukpo, Katsina-Ala, and all other 19 local government areas.
3. Review statewide account requests across all registered schools from the **Account Requests** console.
4. Broadcast executive directives and monitor TRCN teacher compliance across Benue State.

## 9. Troubleshooting & Support

- **"Invalid credentials or account pending approval":** If your credentials are correct but you cannot log in, your account request may still be pending review by your principal.
- **"Session Expired":** For security reasons, inactive sessions expire after a standard duration. Please return to the Gateway and sign in again.
- **Technical Hotline:** Contact the campus administrative hotline at `+234 811 523 1834` or email `ict@bummpt.edu.ng`.
